01 / The federation

Europe's
anti-gravity
agency.

When the Foundation for European Anti-Gravity Research closed in 2042, a continent was left without a voice in the sky. FEISAR answered.

Established in 2036 and designated Europe's primary anti-gravity research agency, FEISAR is funded by the member states of the European Federation. Every chassis we build carries components from every one of them.

We have never chased top speed. We engineer control — the kind that turns rookies into champions and has earned FEISAR the most loyal following in anti-gravity racing.
